Known Gaps

  • No semantic labels are embedded in either DXF; names and meaning must live in the sidecar data.
  • The current report does not attempt to generate a final AviationWorld.
  • Displaced-threshold geometry is intentionally deferred in this slice.
  • The current report only scans OFMX designated points for fix-position reconciliation; it does not yet parse navaids or shape extensions.
  • The current hand-authored VFR circuit omits the eastern holding-point join used by the charted NE reporting-point route; that geometry is still pending DXF update.
  • SECTOR WHISKEY and SECTOR ECHO are not yet modeled as boundary-sector constructs with the corresponding CTR cut-outs. A provisional sketch now exists on the VFR Op Sectors layer in the working airspace DXF and can be shown as a render overlay, but the runtime/model still misplaces SENDER DOBL and KALSDORF as interior reporting points instead of CTR-boundary intersections.

Airspace Notes

  • LOWG 2: OFMX curve vertices are present; the map currently renders straight-segment approximation.
