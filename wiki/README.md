# TWR2 Wiki

Shared knowledge base for the TWR2 project. Updated by both human and AI contributors as work progresses.

## Structure

- [Domain](domain/) -- ATC domain knowledge, models, concepts
- [Data Sources](data-sources/) -- What data we have, where it comes from, what it contains
- [Design Decisions](design-decisions/) -- Point-in-time records of decisions made and why

## Conventions

- Files are markdown, organised by topic
- Design decisions are dated and not updated retroactively -- new decisions supersede old ones
- Domain pages are living documents -- kept current as understanding evolves
- When committing or ending a session, update relevant wiki pages
