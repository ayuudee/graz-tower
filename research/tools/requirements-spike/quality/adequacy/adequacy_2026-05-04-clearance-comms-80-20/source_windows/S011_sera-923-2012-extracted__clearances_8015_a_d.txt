1985: SERA.8015 Air traffic control clearances
1986: (a) Air traffic control clearances shall be based solely on the requirements for providing air traffic control service.
1987: (b) Operation subject to clearance
1988: (1) An air traffic control clearance shall be obtained prior to operating a controlled flight, or a portion of a flight as a
1989: controlled flight. Such clearance shall be requested through the submission of a flight plan to an air traffic control
1990: unit.
1991: (2) The pilot-in-command of an aircraft shall inform ATC if an air traffic control clearance is not satisfactory. In such
1992: cases, ATC will issue an amended clearance, if practicable.
1993: (3) Whenever an aircraft has requested a clearance involving priority, a report explaining the necessity for such
1994: priority shall be submitted, if requested by the appropriate air traffic control unit.
1995: (4) Potential reclearance in flight. If, prior to departure, it is anticipated that, depending on fuel endurance and subject to
1996: reclearance in flight, a decision may be taken to proceed to a revised destination aerodrome, the appropriate air
1997: traffic control units shall be so notified by the insertion in the flight plan of information concerning the revised
1998: route (where known) and the revised destination.
1999: (5) An aircraft operated on a controlled aerodrome shall not taxi on the manoeuvring area without clearance from
2000: the aerodrome control tower and shall comply with any instructions given by that unit.
2001: (c) Clearances for transonic flight
2002: (1) The air traffic control clearance relating to the transonic acceleration phase of a supersonic flight shall extend at
2003: least to the end of that phase.

2015: (d) Contents of clearances
2016: An air traffic control clearance shall indicate:
2017: (1) aircraft identification as shown in the flight plan;

2019: (3) route of flight;
2020: (4) level(s) of flight for the entire route or part thereof and changes of levels if required;
