4917: 4.4 TAXI INSTRUCTIONS
4918:
4919: 4.4.1
4920: Taxi instruction issued by a controller will always contain a clearance limit, which is the point
4921: at which the aircraft must stop until further permission to proceed is given. For departing aircraft, the clearance
4922: limit will normally be the taxi-holding point of the runway in use, but it may be any other position on the
4923: aerodrome depending on the prevailing traffic circumstances.
4924: 4.4.2
4925: When a taxi clearance contains a taxi limit beyond a runway, it shall contain an explicit
4926: clearance to cross, or an instruction to hold short of that runway.
