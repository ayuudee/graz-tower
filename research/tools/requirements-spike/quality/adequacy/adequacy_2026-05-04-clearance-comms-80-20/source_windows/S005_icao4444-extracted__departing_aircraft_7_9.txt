9034: from this order of priority to facilitate the maximum number of departures with the least average delay. Factors which

9062:
9063: Except as provided in 7.11 and Chapter 5, Section 5.8, a departing aircraft will not normally be permitted to commence

9094: Take-off clearance
9095:
9096: 7.9.3.1 Take-off clearance may be issued to an aircraft when there is reasonable assurance that the separation in
9097: 7.9.2, or prescribed in accordance with 7.11, will exist when the aircraft commences take-off.
9098: 7.9.3.2 When an ATC clearance is required prior to take-off, the take-off clearance shall not be issued until the
9099: ATC clearance has been transmitted to and acknowledged by the aircraft concerned. The ATC clearance shall be
9100: forwarded to the aerodrome control tower with the least possible delay after receipt of a request made by the tower or
9101: prior to such request if practicable.
9102: 7.9.3.3 The expression TAKE-OFF shall only be used in radiotelephony when an aircraft is cleared for take-off or
9103: when cancelling a take-off clearance.
9104: Note.— The expression TORA, pronounced TOR-AH, may be used to indicate take-off run available.
9105: 7.9.3.4 Subject to 7.9.3.2, the take-off clearance shall be issued when the aircraft is ready for take-off and at or
9106: approaching the departure runway, and the traffic situation permits. To reduce the potential for misunderstanding, the
9107: take-off clearance shall include the designator of the departure runway.
9108: 7.9.3.5 In the interest of expediting traffic, a clearance for immediate take-off may be issued to an aircraft before it
9109: enters the runway. On acceptance of such clearance the aircraft shall taxi out to the runway and take off in one continuous
9110: movement.
