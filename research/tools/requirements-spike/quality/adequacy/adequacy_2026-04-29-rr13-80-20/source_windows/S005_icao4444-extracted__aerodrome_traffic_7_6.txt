8726: 7.6.2 Designated positions of aircraft in the aerodrome traffic and taxi circuits
8727: The following positions of aircraft in the traffic and taxi circuits are the positions where aircraft normally receive
8728: aerodrome control tower clearances. Aircraft should be watched closely as they approach these positions so that proper
8729: clearances may be issued without delay. Where practicable, all clearances should be issued without waiting for aircraft to
8730: initiate the call.
8731: Position 1. Aircraft initiates call to taxi for departing flight. Runway-in-use information and taxi clearances given.
8732: Position 2. If there is conflicting traffic, the departing aircraft will be held at this position. Engine run-up will, when
8733: required, normally be performed here.
8734: Position 3. Take-off clearance is issued here, if not practicable at position 2.
8735: Position 4. Clearance to land is issued here as practicable.
8736: Position 5. Clearance to taxi to apron is issued here.
8737: Position 6. Parking information issued here, if necessary.
8738: Note 1.— Arriving aircraft executing an instrument approach procedure will normally enter the traffic circuit on
8739: final except when visual manoeuvring to the landing runway is required.

8752: TAXI CLEARANCE
8753: 
8754: 7.6.3.1.1.1 Prior to issuing a taxi clearance, the controller shall determine where the aircraft concerned is parked.
8755: Taxi clearances shall contain concise instructions and adequate information so as to assist the flight crew to follow the
8756: correct taxi routes, to avoid collision with other aircraft or objects and to minimize the potential for the aircraft
8757: inadvertently entering an active runway.
8758: 7.6.3.1.1.2 When a taxi clearance contains a taxi limit beyond a runway, it shall contain an explicit clearance to
8759: cross or an instruction to hold short of that runway.

8805: 7.6.3.1.1.3 The appropriate ATS authority should whenever practicable publish in the national AIP standard taxi
8806: routes to be used at an aerodrome. Standard taxi routes should be identified by appropriate designators and should be used
8807: in taxi clearances.
8808: 7.6.3.1.1.4 Where standard taxi routes have not been published, a taxi route should, whenever possible, be
8809: described by use of taxiway and runway designators. Other relevant information, such as an aircraft to follow or give way
8810: to, shall also be provided to a taxiing aircraft.
